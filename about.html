MCGATEWAYOUR STORY

The story behind the gateway.

A community project built to give Minecraft Bedrock players an easier way into the worlds they want to explore.

THE STORY SO FAR

Built for the players who wanted another way in.

MCGateway started as a practical solution to console server access. What began with one bot has grown into a community-run gateway, complete with a Hub, backend systems, and a fleet of access accounts.

THE PURPOSE

The gateway between you and your server

On many consoles, custom Minecraft Bedrock servers are less straightforward to join than on PC or mobile. MCGateway creates a joinable world through an Xbox friend account. Add a bot, enter MCGateway Hub, then choose the server you want to visit. The gateway is free and does not ask for your Microsoft password or require DNS changes.

Add an access bot → Join MCGateway Hub → Enter a server → Transfer
A BRIEF HISTORY

From one bot to a network

  1. June 2026
    An idea becomes a project

    After a Minecraft update broke transfer tooling, the project was built around an independent, reliable, free access method.

  2. June 20, 2026
    MCGateway opens to the public

    The service launches with one access bot and an early Hub.

  3. July 2026
    Rapid community growth

    Recorded transfer attempts accelerate as players and content creators spread the word.

  4. Later in 2026
    A full gateway ecosystem

    A 16-account fleet, network monitoring, community features, and official guides support the growing service.

  5. Today
    Continuing to improve the gateway

    The team continues improving bot availability, joining guides, service reliability, and the Hub experience.

THE INFRASTRUCTURE

Built to stay useful

Access bots

A public fleet of Xbox friend accounts provides routes into the Hub, with capacity and availability shown on the Bots page.

Network statistics

Recorded activity helps power the statistics page, improve reliability, and support player features.

Server Codes

Short codes simplify entering known destinations, with requests handled through the official Discord.

Privacy by design

Public statistics are aggregated; private destinations and player records are not automatically published.

MEET THE CREATOR

Built by JaxDoesIt

Contributions from builders, testers, and creators helped it reach a wider audience.
