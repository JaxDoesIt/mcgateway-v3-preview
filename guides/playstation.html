MCGATEWAYPLAYSTATION GUIDE

Join custom servers on PlayStation.

Connect Minecraft to a Microsoft account, find an MCGateway gamertag in Minecraft Friends, and join on PS4 or PS5.

All guides›PlayStation
BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Get ready to connect.

Minecraft needs your Microsoft account linked for cross-platform friends. Console multiplayer typically requires appropriate PlayStation Plus access.

STEP 1

Sign in with Microsoft

  1. Launch MinecraftChoose Sign in with a Microsoft account if you are not already connected.
  2. Use the displayed codeFollow the QR or web-link instructions shown by Minecraft.
  3. Complete sign-inUse the Microsoft account you want associated with Minecraft.
  4. Confirm your gamertagReturn to the game and confirm your account appears.
STEP 2

Add an MCGateway friend

  1. Open Play and FriendsSearch for a player from Minecraft, not your PlayStation Network friends list.
  2. Find Cross-Platform FriendsLook for the player search interface on your version.
  3. Enter the Xbox gamertagCopy the available MCGateway bot name from below.
  4. Wait for acceptanceAllow up to one minute, then join the Hub from Friends.

IN THE HUB

Choose your destination

  1. Open the Friends tabAfter the bot adds you back, open Play and find the world named MCGateway Hub.
  2. Enter the HubSelect the joinable friend world and wait for the lobby to load.
  3. Open the menuInteract with the robot or use the Ender Pearl provided in the Hub.
  4. Add your serverEnter a Bedrock server IP address and port, or a Server Code, then connect.
TROUBLESHOOTING

PlayStation help

Should I search by a PlayStation ID?

No. Search by the Xbox/Microsoft gamertag inside Minecraft.

Microsoft account linking fails. What now?

Follow Minecraft’s official PlayStation linking help. PSN accounts can have existing Microsoft account associations.

Does MCGateway replace PlayStation Plus?

No. Platform multiplayer subscription and account requirements still apply.
