ABOUT THESE NUMBERS

What these numbers mean

Total transfers

A transfer is an attempt to reach a destination. Offline servers, wrong ports, or access restrictions can still block the final join.

Unique players

Distinct players who have actually entered MCGateway Hub, not every player who has added a bot.

Tracked servers

Unique server destinations in service history. This count never exposes private destinations or addresses.

Recent activity

Rolling aggregates from the Warehouse, using the latest published 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day periods.
