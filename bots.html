AFTER ADDING A BOT

What happens next?

  1. Open Minecraft BedrockSelect Play, then Friends on the device you are using.
  2. Send a friend requestEnter the exact gamertag and allow roughly 20 to 60 seconds for a response.
  3. Join MCGateway HubLook under joinable Friends worlds. If it does not appear, refresh your Friends list.
  4. Choose your destinationOpen the Hub menu with the robot or Ender Pearl, then enter a Bedrock IP and port or Server Code.
