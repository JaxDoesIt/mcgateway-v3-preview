MCGATEWAYPRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

What MCGateway stores, how its public information works, and how to contact the team about privacy.

SERVICE PRIVACY

Clear about what we keep.

MCGateway uses service history to keep the gateway working and its features useful. Your passwords are not part of the joining process.

OVERVIEW

Service information and history

MCGateway runs access bots, an in-game Hub, a website, Discord services, and a Warehouse backend. Player and service history can be retained to provide persistent features, monitor reliability, investigate issues, and maintain accurate statistics. MCGateway does not sell player information.

ACCOUNT SECURITY

Your passwords stay yours

MCGateway does not ask for your Microsoft or Discord password. Optional Minecraft-to-Discord linking uses a temporary code rather than collecting your account credentials. Never provide passwords to support staff.

INFORMATION USED

How the gateway operates

Service records can include Minecraft identifiers required to recognize returning players, Hub activity, transfer events, saved settings, server history, friend relationships, service-health records, and optional Discord links. Aggregated activity supports lifetime totals and reliability analysis.

SERVER DESTINATIONS

Private stays private

Destination addresses used for transfers may be retained for operation, history, and support. A server in MCGateway history does not automatically become publicly listed. Public features may show destinations separately confirmed for public use.

WEBSITE STATISTICS

What visitors can see

The website publishes service-level totals such as transfers, unique Hub players, tracked server counts, recent activity, Hub population, gateway status, and bot availability. Private credentials and player records are not intended to appear in the public website.

VIDEO TUTORIALS

Third-party video content

Selecting an embedded YouTube tutorial causes the browser to connect to YouTube. You can choose to open videos on YouTube instead. Google and YouTube policies may apply to those third-party requests.

BOT CAPACITY

Inactive friendships

To preserve limited friend slots, MCGateway may remove inactive access-bot friendships after about one month. Returning players can add an available bot again.

RETENTION & SAFETY

Keeping the service reliable

Retention can differ by record type. Historical and aggregate records may be kept for reliability, feature continuity, moderation, and accurate service history. Serious abuse can be investigated using relevant records.

QUESTIONS

Contact the team

For privacy questions or requests involving information associated with your MCGateway account, contact support through the official Discord or mcgatewaycontact@gmail.com.

This page reflects the official MCGateway privacy information published November 5, 2026. Confirm any future changes in advertising, analytics, or data practices before publication.
