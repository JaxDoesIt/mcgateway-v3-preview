NEED A HAND?

Questions before you join?

Common questions before you start, answered without the guesswork.

MCGateway Hub isn’t showing in Friends. What now?

Make sure the bot accepted your friend request, check multiplayer permissions, and restart Minecraft if needed. You can also try another available bot.

Do I have to change DNS settings?

No. MCGateway works through Minecraft’s Friends tab, without changing your console network settings or installing another console app.

How do I connect after entering the Hub?

Use the robot or Ender Pearl to open the Hub menu. Enter the Bedrock address and port, or a Server Code, then connect.
