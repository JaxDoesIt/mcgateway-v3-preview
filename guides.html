THE SHORT VERSION

How MCGateway works

  1. Use Minecraft: Bedrock EditionSign in to your Microsoft account and ensure multiplayer permissions are enabled.
  2. Add one available botPick a gamertag from the live access fleet.
  3. Join MCGateway HubAfter the automatic friend acceptance, enter the world through your Friends tab.
  4. Open the gateway menuInteract with the robot or throw the provided Ender Pearl, then enter your Bedrock server or Server Code.

STILL STUCK?

Common connection questions

Does MCGateway require DNS changes?

No. You join through a friend world in Minecraft Bedrock.

I cannot see MCGateway Hub. What should I try?

Allow up to one minute for acceptance, check the current bot list, and restart Minecraft if the Friends list has not refreshed.

Can I join Java Edition servers?

Only when the destination has Bedrock compatibility, such as a supported cross-play setup.

Do I need a subscription?

Depending on your console, its online multiplayer service may require a separate subscription. MCGateway itself is free.
